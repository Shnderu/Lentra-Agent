# Lentra Architecture Constitution

> This document is the single source of architectural truth for the Lentra project.
> Any code that contradicts this document is considered legacy.

---

## 1. Product Identity

**Lentra = AI Market Intelligence OS** for the rental market of South-East Asia.

Lentra is **NOT**:
- a marketplace
- a listings aggregator
- a simple housing search

Core value:
- **Price Intelligence**
- **Risk Analysis**
- **Duplicate Detection**
- **Area Intelligence**
- **AI Decision Support**
